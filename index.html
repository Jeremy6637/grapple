Controls

◀ ▶ run · JUMP jump, tap again in the air to double jump · hold SWING to fire the chain at the nearest girder or blue drone

← → or A D run · Space jump, again in the air to double jump · hold Shift or K to fire the chain (click and hold to aim it yourself) · ↑ ↓ reel · R restart · M mute · N music

Moves

Swing: pump back and forth, then let go on the up-swing to fly. Press Jump as you let go for extra height.

Enemies: land on them to stomp, smash into them at speed, or quick-tap Swing on any drone to yank it down. Swing at a thug to fling him into the air. Anything you yank wrecks the enemies it hits.

Walls: hit a wall fast to run up it, jump off it to climb.

Score

Coins grabbed in a row are worth more. Everything you earn in the air is multiplied by your chain until you land. Big air, fast swings and a flawless run pay bonuses, and every level gives a rank from S to D.
