GRAPPLE ZERO-G

Feel prototype. Shapes only, no art yet. Two tethers, no engines.

Controls

Hold your phone upright: the ship flies up the screen and your thumbs stay below it. On a keyboard, A/← hooks a rock on one side, D/→ the other. Hold to stay on, release to let go.

Upgrades

Every run starts as a bare cockpit. Each gate bolts on the next part. Every hull hit knocks the newest part off: fly back through the glowing salvage to get it back, or earn it again at the next gate.

Rocks

Anchor: swing around it
Flywheel: winds you up, let go to fling
Gravity well: bends your path, skim close for a boost
Rubble: yank and throw it
Red: explodes. Hook one close for a blast-jump
Ice: shatters into rubble
Ore: breaks into gold
Hunter drone: chases you
Turret: shoots slow bolts

How it flies

  • While a hook holds, the tether reels in. The tighter you circle, the faster you go. Let go to fling yourself.
  • Slingshot: hook a big rock on each side and hold both. The tethers charge (watch the ring). Let go of either to launch between them.
  • Flail: anchor one tether on a big rock and hook a small rock with the other. Swing, then let the small one go to throw it.
  • Hull: when your ship glows red, you're going fast enough that hitting a rock costs one hull pip (◆). Your ship veers round big rocks by itself, so most hits come from swinging in too tight. Three hits ends the run, but the hull repairs one pip after 10 seconds without a hit (the next pip fills in on the HUD). Red rocks and small rubble never hurt: they explode or crumble, and so does the rock you're tethered to.
  • Enemies: there's no gun. Ram a drone or turret at speed, yank a drone into your hull, throw a rock at them, or blow them up. A drone flies straight at you, so swing round a big rock and it crashes. Touching one while you're slow costs hull, and so does a turret bolt.
  • Stay ahead of the collapse chasing you.
